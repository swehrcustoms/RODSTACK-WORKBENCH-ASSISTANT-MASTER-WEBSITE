# Marketing asset reference

Imported from the RodStack Workbench Website Asset Pack into `public/marketing/`.

## Provenance

Photographic files in `public/marketing/photos/` are crops of one commissioned generated workshop scene. They are not separate photos of RodStack customers or shops. Captions on the site say so.

Feature illustrations in `public/marketing/feature-illustrations/` are vector mockups with matching WebP renders. They show sample data. They are not screenshots of the live Workbench. Each product figure on the site carries a sample-data label.

Site graphics in `public/marketing/site-graphics/` support pricing, the product family, and the feature map. The pricing graphic does not replace the live comparison table.

Official logos were copied from `RodStack_AI_Workbench_Assistant` `public/`:

- `RS Logo.png` — small mark, favicon, mobile header
- `RodStack Logo V2.png` — header and footer
- `RodStack Logo V1.png` — homepage hero

Logos were not redrawn. Black logo backgrounds are screened onto the navy page with `mix-blend-mode: screen`.
